# Zone 20 Grass + Water Starters Alt

Info:
	This is useful for shiny hunting the grass and water starters in Zone 20 as well as some of the surrounding spawns. This is similar to the original, except it enters the zone in an attempt to get more starter spawns. Toggle the macro after fast traveling to Zone 20 and make sure the map is zoomed all the way out.

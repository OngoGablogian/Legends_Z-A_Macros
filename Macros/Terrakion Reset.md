# Terrakion Reset:

<img src="/Macro-Images/Terrakion_Reset.gif" />

### <ins>Info:</ins>
This is useful for shiny hunting Terrakion in [Hyperspace Lumiose](https://www.serebii.net/legendsz-a/hyperspacelumiose.shtml). Toggle the macro while standing on top of the teleporter that's located on the roof where Terrakion spawns.

----
